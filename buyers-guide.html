Buyer’s Guide

Buying a home is one of the biggest decisions you will make. Here is the path, one step at a time.

Part 1 of 3

Search

  1. 01

    Find an agent

    I know the neighborhoods and track comparable homes, on and off market.

  2. 02

    Get pre-approved

    Learn what the bank will lend before you look. I can connect you to a mortgage broker.

  3. 03

    Visit properties

    See a range of homes. The neighborhood matters as much as the floor plan.

Part 2 of 3

Offer and contract

  1. 04

    Co-op or condo

    A condo is a deed and more flexibility. A co-op is shares in the building and a board review.

  2. 05

    Negotiate

    Agree on price and terms with the seller. I advise on pricing throughout.

  3. 06

    Review

    Your attorney reviews the contract, the building’s financials and board minutes.

  4. 07

    Sign the contract

    Sign and put down your deposit, then apply for your loan and appraisal.

Part 3 of 3

Closing

  1. 08

    Prepare your board package

    For a co-op, I help you prepare the board package and the interview.

  2. 09

    Closing costs

    Plan for attorney, bank and building fees, plus the Mansion Tax from $1M.

  3. 10

    Approval and closing

    Board approval, clearance to close, then a final walk-through.

  4. 11

    Welcome home

    Congratulations. You are now a homeowner.
