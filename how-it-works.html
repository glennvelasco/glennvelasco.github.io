How itworks

A clear path, one step at a time.

  1. Step 01

    Find an agent

    I know the neighborhoods and share real-time insight on comparable homes, on and off market.

  2. Step 02

    Get pre-approved

    Know how much the bank will lend before you look. I can connect you to a mortgage broker.

  3. Step 03

    Visit properties

    Viewings and open houses across areas and property types. The neighborhood matters as much as the floor plan.

  4. Step 04

    Co-op or condo

    A condo is a deed and more flexibility. A co-op is shares in the building and a board review.

  5. Step 05

    Negotiate

    Agree on price and terms with the seller. I advise on pricing throughout.

  6. Step 06

    Review

    Your attorney reviews the contract, building financials and board minutes.

  7. Step 07

    Sign the contract

    Sign and put down your deposit, then complete your loan application and appraisal.

  8. Step 08

    Prepare your board package

    For a co-op, I help prepare your board package and your interview.

  9. Step 09

    Closing costs

    Plan for attorney, bank and building fees, plus the Mansion Tax from $1M.

  10. Step 10

    Approval and closing

    Board approval, clearance to close, then a final walk-through.

  11. Step 11

    Welcome home

    Congratulations. You are now a homeowner.
