FAQs About your home valuation
Is it free?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation to list your home.

What happens after I send this?

Glenn reviews your details and contacts you by email or phone to talk about your home and its value.

What affects a home’s value in New York City?

Recent sales of comparable homes nearby, size and layout, floor and exposure, condition, building type (condo or co-op), building amenities, and monthly charges such as common charges, maintenance and taxes.

Is this the same as an appraisal?

No. A valuation from an agent is a market opinion based on comparable sales. A formal appraisal is done by a licensed appraiser, usually for a lender.

How is this different from an online home value estimator?

An online home value estimator runs public records through a formula. Glenn looks at your specific apartment and building (layout, floor, light, condition and monthly charges) and compares it with recent sales nearby, the way a comparative market analysis does.
