About Glenn

Born in Bolivia, Glenn grew up throughout the States and Europe, living in Washington DC, Milan, Paris and Barcelona, before making New York City home. He knows what it takes to settle into a new home, so clients relocating trust him to treat their move with that same care. Today he guides his clients through New York in four languages.

His professional background in property management, as well as his experience in the New York start up scene equip him with unique insight that he uses when working with investors. Glenn is devoted to providing attentive and personalized real estate solutions for clients looking to buy, sell or rent property in New York City.

More than an agent, Glenn aims to be a trusted advisor, partner, confidant and friend to his clients.

Glenn works alongside Miky Bonazzoli on the M Team at Compass. Between them they work in English, Spanish, Italian, French and Hebrew, and many of their clients are moving from abroad or buying from overseas, so there's a team behind every deal.
