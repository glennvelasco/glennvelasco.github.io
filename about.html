About Glenn

Born in Santa Cruz, Bolivia, Glenn grew up throughout the states and Europe living in different cities such as Washington DC, Milan, Paris, and Barcelona, ultimately setting his sights on New York City where he now specializes in helping clients navigate the jungle that is NYC real estate. His travels have led him to become fluent in an array of languages including English, Spanish, Italian, and French. His professional background in property management, as well as his experience in the New York start up scene equip him with unique insight that he uses when working with investors. Glenn is fluent in diversity of context from the experiences he has lived and in diversity of communication from the languages he speaks, enabling him to empathize and anticipate the needs of any client while ensuring that they are informed and at ease.

With an appreciation for aesthetics, real estate, and a deep passion for helping others, Glenn is a driven real estate agent devoted to providing attentive and personalized real estate solutions for clients looking to buy, sell or rent property in New York City. As a motivated leader and agent, Glenn always maintains the highest standards of professionalism and commitment with the goal of offering his clients an experience where quality, service and results are uncompromised. More than an agent, Glenn aims to be a trusted advisor, partner, confidant and friend to his clients.
