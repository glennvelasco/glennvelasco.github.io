About Glenn

Born in Santa Cruz, Bolivia, Glenn grew up throughout the states and Europe living in different cities such as Washington DC, Milan, Paris, and Barcelona, ultimately setting his sights on New York City where he now specializes in helping clients navigate the jungle that is NYC real estate. His travels have led him to become fluent in an array of languages including English, Spanish, Italian, and French.

His professional background in property management, as well as his experience in the New York start up scene equip him with unique insight that he uses when working with investors. Glenn is devoted to providing attentive and personalized real estate solutions for clients looking to buy, sell or rent property in New York City.

More than an agent, Glenn aims to be a trusted advisor, partner, confidant and friend to his clients.
